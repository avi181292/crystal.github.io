Our Story

Strung by hand, from our home to yours

Lunette Stones started at our kitchen table with a handful of rose quartz and a spool of elastic cord. What began as gifts for friends slowly grew into a small home studio, where every bracelet is still strung one bead at a time.

We work with trusted crystal suppliers and check each stone by hand before it becomes part of a piece — so what reaches your wrist is exactly what we'd wear ourselves.
